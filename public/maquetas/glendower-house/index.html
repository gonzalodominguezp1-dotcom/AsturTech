Welcome

Your home in the heart of the Norman Way

Glendower House is a family-run bed and breakfast on the Wexford Road at Portersland, on the edge of New Ross. Every room looks out over our large mature gardens.

Leave the car in our secure off-street car park and walk into town for the Dunbrody Ship, the Ros Tapestry and plenty of shops, restaurants and pubs. Families and four-legged friends are always welcome.
